## Quick copy (2-slide version)

## SLIDE 1 — What is Scenario Builder?

**Scenario Builder**  
*One platform for scenario-based training and assessment*

- **One place** for running exercises, tracking assignments, and viewing analytics — no more scattered tools or spreadsheets.
- **Real-time exercises**: pick a scenario, work through incidents step-by-step with a timer, save progress, and submit for grading.
- **Clear outcomes**: assignment history, per-incident scores, cohort comparison, and exportable reports (e.g. PDF).
- **Secure and flexible**: identity and access via your existing identity provider; works across training and pilot environments.

**Tagline:** *From scenario to score — one coherent experience.*

---

## SLIDE 2 — Why it matters

**Strategic value**

- **Stop reinventing** — Reuse the same platform for new scenarios and clients instead of building one-off training UIs.
- **Build lasting IP** — A single, reusable flow (scenarios → exercises → assignments → analytics) becomes your standard for training products.
- **Scale with confidence** — One architecture for many domains: incident response, assessments, cohort analytics — same frontend, different use cases.
- **Trust and compliance** — Centralised identity, secure access, and consistent integration simplify security and audit.

**Bottom line:** *We deliver a production-ready training platform, not just a project deliverable — scalable digital foundations for the long term.*
